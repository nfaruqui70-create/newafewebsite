What Your Child Will Learn

Our program helps children build a strong Islamic foundation while developing the academic skills they need for kindergarten. Through play, stories, songs, crafts, and interactive learning, students will explore both faith and early education.

Children will learn:

  • Love for Allah and His creation
  • Stories of the Prophets and important Islamic values
  • Daily duas and Islamic manners
  • Short Quran surahs and introductory Arabic
  • Letters, sounds, numbers, shapes, and colors
  • Early reading, writing, and math skills
  • Creativity, problem-solving, and social development through hands-on activities
